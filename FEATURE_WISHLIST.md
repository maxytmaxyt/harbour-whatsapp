# 💡 Feature Wishlist

This is the community wishlist for **harbour-whatsapp**.  
Got an idea? Open a [Feature Request issue](https://github.com/maxytmaxyt/harbour-whatsapp/issues/new?template=feature_request.md) or drop a comment here!

---

## 🗳️ How to request a feature

1. Check the list below — maybe it's already planned!
2. Open a [💡 Feature Request](https://github.com/maxytmaxyt/harbour-whatsapp/issues/new?template=feature_request.md) issue
3. Describe your idea clearly — *what* it does, *why* it's useful, and *how* it could work
4. Others can 👍 your issue to help me prioritize
